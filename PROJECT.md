# CannabisAI

## Mission
CannabisAI is an AI business operating assistant built for legal cannabis
dispensaries and cannabis brands.

## Core Users
- Dispensary owners
- Managers
- Budtenders
- Cannabis brands
- Marketing teams

## Core Systems

### AI Assistant
Answers business questions and helps staff work faster.

### Products
Product catalog and product knowledge.

### Inventory
Inventory levels, low-stock alerts, and product movement.

### Marketing
Captions, campaigns, product descriptions, and content planning.

### Compliance
Flags potentially problematic marketing language.

### Business Intelligence
Sales, product performance, and operational insights.

## Development Rules

1. `main` is the stable branch.
2. Do not directly experiment on `main`.
3. Create a feature branch before building a feature.
4. Test changes before merging.
5. Do not delete another developer's work.
6. Document major architectural changes.
7. Never put API keys directly into source code.
8. Use environment variables for secrets.
9. CannabisAI does not provide legal or medical advice.
