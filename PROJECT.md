# CannabisAI

## Mission
CannabisAI is an AI business operating assistant built for legal cannabis
dispensaries and cannabis brands.

## Core Users
- Dispensary owners
- Managers
- Budtenders
- Cannabis brands
- Marketing teams

## Core Systems

### AI Assistant
Answers business questions and helps staff work faster.

### Products
Product catalog and product knowledge.

### Inventory
Inventory levels, low-stock alerts, and product movement.

### Marketing
Captions, campaigns, product descriptions, and content planning.

### Compliance
Flags potentially problematic marketing language.

### Business Intelligence
Sales, product performance, and operational insights.

## Development Rules

1. `main` is the stable branch.
2. Do not directly experiment on `main`.
3. Create a feature branch before building a feature.
4. Test changes before merging.
5. Do not delete another developer's work.
6. Document major architectural changes.
7. Never put API keys directly into source code.
8. Use environment variables for secrets.
9. CannabisAI does not provide legal or medical advice.

## Privacy-First LeadFinder

CannabisAI LeadFinder may discover and qualify potential business customers
using publicly available information from sources such as public Reddit
communities, public Telegram communities/channels, and public web pages.

### Privacy Rules

- Public information only.
- Do not access private groups, private channels, or private messages.
- Do not collect passwords, session cookies, authentication tokens, or credentials.
- Do not harvest personal phone numbers or personal contact information.
- Prefer business names, business websites, public business accounts, and public business contact information.
- Collect the minimum information necessary for lead qualification.
- Give users visibility into what information was collected and why.
- Allow stored leads to be deleted.
- Do not automatically send mass messages.
- Outreach requires human approval.
- Respect applicable platform rules and terms.
- Never attempt to bypass platform restrictions or anti-spam systems.
- Privacy and data minimization take priority over lead volume.

### LeadFinder Goal

LeadFinder discovers potentially relevant businesses and communities,
scores their fit for CannabisAI, explains why they may be a good prospect,
and presents the opportunity to the human operator for review.

LeadFinder is a research and qualification tool, not a spam bot.
